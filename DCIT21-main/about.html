About CVSU Market

A campus directory that helps student-owned businesses get found and helps buyers shop local.

General Objectives

  • To develop a web-based platform “CVSU MARKET” designed to give information and enhance promotion and visibility of student-owned businesses inside Cavite State University - Main.
  • To develop and design the screen layout and navigation that allows students to easily navigate the platform.
  • To build the core features of the program that includes registration, business profile, product search, and browsing.

Specific Problems Addressed

Fragmented Communication Channels

Student entrepreneurs currently rely on informal, scattered channels such as personal social media posts, group chats, and word-of-mouth to market their products and services, leading to low visibility and missed sales opportunities.

Lack of a Centralized Platform

CVSU students who want to support fellow student vendors lack a dedicated, centralized directory to easily discover, browse, or search for items offered within the university community.

Inconvenient Product Discovery

Potential buyers are often unaware of existing student businesses, their current inventories, pricing, or ordering procedures due to the lack of an organized campus marketplace interface.

Target Users

CVSU Student Entrepreneurs (Vendors)

Undergraduate and graduate students running micro-businesses, side hustles, or selling goods/services who need a streamlined, centralized platform to showcase their products, manage catalog visibility, and reach potential buyers across the campus community.

CVSU Students (Buyers)

Students looking for convenient access to local products, customized items, campus services, food, or pre-loved goods provided directly by their peers within Cavite State University.

CVSU Faculty and Campus Staff (Secondary Buyers)

University employees interested in discovering and buying products directly from student sellers.

Core System Features & Development Team

  • Registration: Allows users to log in and access the website.
  • Business Profile: Shows the details about the business and their contact information.
  • Product Details: Shows information about the product including product name, prices, description, and images.
  • Search Functionality: Allows the user to search for a product.
  • Product Categories: Organizes products based on their type so users can easily browse.
