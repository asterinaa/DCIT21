ABOUT BUSINESS

CVSU MARKET is a web-based platform designed to enhance promotion and visibility of student-owned businesses inside Cavite State University - Main.

It solves fragmented social media marketing by offering a dedicated, centralized directory for campus buyers.

Students, faculty, and campus staff can easily discover local food, customized items, campus services, and pre-loved goods.

Student entrepreneurs can register, build a business profile, and display product names, prices, descriptions, and images.
